Working Principle

The system begins with a self-test, then continuously reads multiple gas sensors. Readings are compared with a safe threshold. If leakage is detected, local alarms (buzzer, red LED, ISD voice) are triggered, the relay activates for gas cutoff and fan, and the ESP32 logs events to Firebase and can trigger remote voice notifications via IFTTT. Energy-efficient sleep mode reduces power consumption when idle. Data is logged for remote monitoring and analysis.
