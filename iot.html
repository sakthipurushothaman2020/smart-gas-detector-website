How to connect

  1. Create a Firebase project → Realtime Database.
  2. In your ESP32 code, POST/PATCH readings to: https://YOUR_PROJECT.firebaseio.com/gas.json
  3. Set FIREBASE_URL in script.js to your DB URL.
